# ADR-001: Complete Spanish and Simplified Chinese chrome in one V1

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-08-31

## Context

Idle manager chrome (settings, start/stop, empty states, and wipe/delete/clear-session confirms) exists only in Portuguese and English.
Game documents are third-party sites and stay in the game's own language.
The verified problem is current operators whose working language is not PT or EN misreading chrome, especially destructive confirms.
This is not a public-site, first-run, or new-region launch problem.

The operator asked for more than PT and EN, naming Spanish and Chinese as examples.
Steam store languages split Spanish (Spain vs Latin America) and Chinese (Simplified vs Traditional).
Wikipedia/Ethnologue 2026 ranks Mandarin and Spanish among the largest languages by total speakers, and notes that "Chinese" is not always one variety.

## Decision Drivers

- Need: chrome confirms and labels must be readable in the operator's working language
- Primary user: current operators who already run isolated accounts; PT/EN users keep their chrome
- Success: ship-time checklist that every chrome string, including destructive confirms, is in the selected locale; PT/EN still match current copy
- V1 extra languages: one Spanish locale and Simplified Chinese
- Out of V1: public site, README, game documents, OS-language auto-pick, operator-typed names
- New workspaces still default to Portuguese
- Language is still chosen in Settings

## Decision

V1 ships **complete chrome dictionaries** for **one Spanish locale** and **Simplified Chinese**, in the **same release**.

- Every existing chrome string, including wipe/delete/clear-session confirms, has Spanish and Simplified Chinese copy.
- Settings offers those languages next to Portuguese and English.
- PT and EN copy stay as they are.
- Switching language does not start, stop, or wipe isolated sessions.
- Operator-typed tab and account names are not rewritten.
- Unknown saved locales keep today's fallback to Portuguese.
- A language is not offered if it cannot pass the full chrome checklist.

Traditional Chinese, a second Spanish locale, the public site, README, game-page translation, and OS auto-pick stay out.

## Alternatives Considered

### Spanish complete pack first, Simplified Chinese next

- **User value:** Spanish operators unblocked sooner; Chinese is not rushed.
- **Costs/risks:** Chinese slips; two checklists.
- **Why not selected:** The operator chose both languages in one V1.

### Both in V1, withheld until destructive confirms are native-reviewed

- **User value:** Highest-cost strings are not guessable.
- **Costs/risks:** Ship date slips; a language may be missing at V1.
- **Why not selected:** The operator chose complete packs now, with the checklist as the quality bar.

### Include the public site or OS auto-pick

- **User value:** Discovery and first launch match the new languages.
- **Costs/risks:** Reopens product-website ADR-002; changes the Portuguese default.
- **Why not selected:** Need and non-goals locked chrome-only, Settings picker, Portuguese default.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Current operators can read chrome and destructive confirms in Spanish or Simplified Chinese without changing how sessions work.
- PT and EN operators are not asked to move.
- Site and game documents stay out of this packet.

### Negative and trade-offs

- Traditional Chinese and Spain-specific Spanish wait.
- Two dictionaries can ship with uneven tone if the checklist is treated as "non-empty" rather than readable.
- First launch still defaults to Portuguese.

### Risks and mitigations

- Incomplete pack offered as a language - Do not show the language in Settings until the checklist passes.
- Operators expect the game to switch language - Game documents stay third-party; chrome-only is explicit in the PRD.
- One Spanish locale feels Spain-only or LatAm-only - Write one Spanish that stays readable in both; do not split locales in V1.

## Reversibility

- Hide a language from Settings if copy fails the checklist or operators cannot read confirms.
- Add Traditional Chinese or a second Spanish locale later if that gap is evidenced.
- Reopen site language only with a product-website decision.

## Follow-ups

- Ship-time checklist for Spanish, Simplified Chinese, Portuguese, and English on an existing workspace.
- Confirm that language switch does not wipe sessions.
